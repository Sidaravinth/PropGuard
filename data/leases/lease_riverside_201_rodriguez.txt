RIVERSIDE LOFTS
RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

Landlord: Riverside Lofts Management Inc.
Tenant: Elena Rodriguez
Premises: Loft 201, Riverside Lofts, 88 River Street, Springfield

§1 TERM OF TENANCY
The tenancy begins on June 1, 2026 and ends on May 31, 2027.

§2 RENT
The Tenant agrees to pay $2,100.00 per month as rent, due on the first (1st) day of each month, by bank transfer to the account designated by the Landlord.

§3 USE OF PREMISES
The Premises shall be used solely as a private residence for the Tenant and immediate family.

§4 MAINTENANCE
4(a) The Landlord is responsible for repairs to the building structure, roof, and building-wide systems.
4(b) For repairs to fixtures and appliances within the Premises, the Tenant shall pay a repair deductible of $150.00 per repair, and the Landlord shall pay the balance.

§5 PETS
Small pets under 25 lbs are permitted with prior written approval of the Landlord.

§6 NOTICE TO VACATE
Either party wishing to end the tenancy at the expiry of the term must give the other party not less than sixty (60) days' written notice.

§7 UTILITIES
The Tenant is responsible for electricity and internet. Water and trash are included in the rent.

Signed on May 20, 2026.
