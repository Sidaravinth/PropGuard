MAPLE COURT APARTMENTS — APARTMENT LEASE

PARTIES: Maple Court Property Holdings LLC (the "Owner") and Marcus Chen (the "Resident").

Article I — Dwelling
1.1 The Owner rents to the Resident Apartment 102 at Maple Court Apartments, 1420 Maple Avenue, Springfield.

Article II — Lease Term
2.1 Start Date: March 1, 2026.
2.2 End Date: February 28, 2027.

Article III — Rent and Charges
3.1 Monthly Rent: $1,650.00, payable in advance on or before the 1st of each month.
3.2 A late charge of 5% of the monthly rent applies to any payment received after the 3rd of the month.

Article IV — Security Deposit
4.1 Resident has paid a security deposit in the amount of $2,000.00, to be held by Owner as security for Resident's performance of this lease.

Article V — Repairs
5.1 Resident shall promptly report any needed repairs to the Owner in writing.
5.2 Resident shall pay a repair deductible of $250.00 per service call for repairs not caused by normal wear and tear; Owner pays any amount above the deductible.

Article VI — Animals
6.1 No pets, animals, birds, or reptiles of any kind are permitted in the dwelling.

Article VII — Termination and Notice
7.1 Resident must give the Owner at least forty-five (45) days' written notice before the End Date if Resident does not intend to renew.
7.2 Owner shall give Resident at least forty-five (45) days' written notice of any proposed change in rent upon renewal.

Executed on February 20, 2026.
