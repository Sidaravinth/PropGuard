RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT — RIVERSIDE LOFTS

This agreement is made between Riverside Lofts Management Inc. ("Landlord") and James O'Connor ("Tenant") for the premises at Loft 202, Riverside Lofts, 88 River Street, Springfield.

1. TERM. This lease begins September 1, 2025 and expires August 31, 2026.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay rent of $1,950.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,950.00, refundable subject to the terms of this lease and applicable law.

4. REPAIRS. Tenant is responsible for the first $200.00 of each repair to appliances or fixtures (the repair deductible). Landlord is responsible for the remainder of the cost of such repairs and for all structural repairs.

5. PETS. Tenant may keep one dog with a pet deposit of $400.00, included in neither rent nor the security deposit.

6. NOTICE. Tenant must provide sixty (60) days' written notice of intent to vacate prior to the expiration of this lease. Following expiration, absent a new written lease, the tenancy shall become a month-to-month holdover tenancy terminable on thirty (30) days' notice.

Signed: August 15, 2025
Landlord: Riverside Lofts Management Inc.
Tenant: James O'Connor
