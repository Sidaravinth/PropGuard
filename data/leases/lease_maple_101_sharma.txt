RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into between Maple Court Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Priya Sharma ("Tenant").

SECTION 1. PREMISES
Landlord leases to Tenant the residential dwelling known as Unit 101, Maple Court Apartments, 1420 Maple Avenue, Springfield ("Premises").

SECTION 2. TERM
The term of this Lease shall commence on January 16, 2026 and shall end on January 15, 2027, unless renewed or terminated earlier in accordance with this Lease.

SECTION 3. RENT
3.1 Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,500.00 (One Thousand Five Hundred Dollars), due on the first day of each calendar month.
3.2 Late Fee. Rent received after the fifth (5th) day of the month shall incur a late fee of $75.00.

SECTION 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord a security deposit of $1,500.00. The security deposit may be applied by Landlord to unpaid rent or to damage to the Premises beyond normal wear and tear. Landlord shall return any remaining balance within thirty (30) days after Tenant vacates.

SECTION 5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
5.1 Landlord shall maintain the structural elements, plumbing, heating and electrical systems in good working order.
5.2 Repair Deductible. For each repair request arising from Tenant's use of the Premises, Tenant shall be responsible for the first $100.00 of the repair cost (the "Repair Deductible"). Landlord shall bear the cost in excess of the Repair Deductible, except where damage is caused by Tenant's negligence, in which case Tenant shall bear the full cost.

SECTION 6. PETS
Tenant may keep one (1) domestic cat at the Premises. Tenant shall pay a non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 and is liable for any damage caused by the pet. No other animals are permitted without Landlord's written consent.

SECTION 7. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL
Either party may terminate this Lease at the end of the term by giving written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the end date. If no notice is given, this Lease shall continue on a month-to-month basis.

SECTION 8. RENEWAL
Landlord may offer Tenant a renewal of this Lease no later than the notice deadline set out in Section 7. Any renewal rent adjustment shall be stated in the written renewal offer.

Tenant: Priya Sharma
Date: January 10, 2026
