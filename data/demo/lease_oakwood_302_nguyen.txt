OAKWOOD TERRACE RESIDENTIAL LEASE

This Lease is made between Oakwood Terrace Residences LP ("Lessor") and Daniel Nguyen ("Lessee").

Clause 1. Premises
Lessor leases to Lessee Unit 302, Oakwood Terrace, 7 Oakwood Drive, Springfield.

Clause 2. Term
The lease term runs from December 1, 2026 through November 30, 2027.

Clause 3. Rent
Lessee shall pay monthly rent of $1,850.00 on the first day of each month.

Clause 4. Maintenance
Lessee shall report maintenance issues promptly. Lessor will arrange repairs within a reasonable time.

Clause 5. Notice
Lessee shall give Lessor written notice of non-renewal no less than sixty (60) days before the end of the term.

Signed November 25, 2026.
Lessor: Oakwood Terrace Residences LP
Lessee: Daniel Nguyen
